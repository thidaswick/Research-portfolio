Cumulative Curriculum

SLIIT

Faculty of Computing

  • March 2026

    Project Proposal

    A Project Proposal is presented to potential sponsors, faculty supervisors and the evaluation panel to establish research feasibility, define problem scope, review literature, and receive formal project approval.

    Marks Allocated : 06 Marks
    6%
  • June 2026

    Progress Presentation I

    Progress Presentation I reviews the 50% completion status of the project. This assessment evaluates initial data preprocessing, early multi-causal model training, and reveals any design or requirement inconsistencies.

    Marks Allocated : 06 Marks
    6%
  • July 2026

    Research Paper

    Describes what you contribute to existing knowledge, giving due recognition to all work that you referred to in making new knowledge. Covers experimental results, Random Forest validation, and explainable AI metrics.

    Marks Allocated : 10 Marks
    10%
  • September 2026

    Progress Presentation II

    Progress Presentation II reviews the 90% completion status demonstration of the project, showcasing end-to-end component integration alongside an academic poster presentation describing the research framework as a whole.

    Marks Allocated : 18 Marks
    18%
  • October 2026

    Website Assessment

    The Website helps to promote our research project and reveals all details related to the project including architectural schematics, team profiles, downloadable deliverables, and domain methodologies.

    Marks Allocated : 02 Marks
    2%
  • November 2026

    Logbook & Status Documents

    Status of the project is validated through the Logbook. This also includes Status Documents 1 & 2, supervisor feedback logs, and weekly sprint progress records across all 4 research components.

    Marks Allocated : 03 Marks
    3%
  • November 2026

    Final Report (Thesis)

    Final Report evaluates the completed project done throughout the year. Marks mentioned below include marks for Individual & Group reports and the complete comprehensive dissertation submission.

    Marks Allocated : 19 Marks
    19%
  • November 2026

    Final Presentation & Viva

    Viva is held individually to assess each member's contribution to the project, model accuracy, architectural convergence, and response to rigorous oral questioning by the academic panel.

    Marks Allocated : 20 Marks
    20%
