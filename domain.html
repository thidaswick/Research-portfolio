Domain

Research domain & methodology

The academic context, problem and approach underpinning the framework.

01 · Literature survey

Background literature

Artificial Intelligence

AI increasingly supports decisions across education, careers and hiring, raising questions of fairness and transparency.

Machine Learning

Supervised learning models such as Random Forest are widely applied to classification problems involving structured career data.

Career Development

Career growth is shaped by many interacting factors — skills, behaviour, learning habits and strategy.

Recruitment

Recruiters face uncertainty in evaluating candidate trust and hiring risk, motivating decision-support tools.

Workforce Transformation

Digital transformation continually reshapes job roles and the skills employers demand.

Automation

Automation and AI change which tasks are replaceable, affecting long-term career safety.

Explainable AI

XAI techniques make model outputs interpretable so users can understand and trust recommendations.

Labour Market Changes

Shifting market trends and technology stacks influence the stability of industries and domains.

02 · Problem & gap

Research problem

Individuals and employers lack integrated, explainable tools that consider multiple causal factors when making career and recruitment decisions in a rapidly changing, AI-influenced labour market.

Research gap

Existing solutions tend to address career guidance, behaviour, industry stability or hiring risk in isolation, often as black boxes. Few combine these perspectives within one explainable framework.

03 · Objectives

Research objectives

  1. 01 Develop an explainable model for interview readiness analysis and career path recommendation.
  2. 02 Predict career impact by considering behavioural factors.
  3. 03 Analyse career safety and industry stability to classify career risk.
  4. 04 Support employers with AI-assisted trust and hiring risk assessment.
  5. 05 Integrate all components into a unified, explainable decision-support framework.

04 · Methodology

Methodology timeline

  1. STAGE 1

    Data Collection

    Multi-source collection of structured career, technical competency, and industry stability metrics.

  2. STAGE 2

    Data Preprocessing

    Cleaning, handling missing values, encoding categorical indicators, and standardization.

  3. STAGE 3

    Feature Engineering

    Extraction and weighting of key features including career strategy, learning time, and replaceability.

  4. STAGE 4

    Model Development

    Architecture development and Random Forest ensemble training for robust classification.

  5. STAGE 5

    Model Evaluation

    Empirical evaluation yielding 85% accuracy on 140 test instances and 65.7% domain stability accuracy.

  6. STAGE 6

    Explainability

    Generating feature-importance rationales and transparent reasoning for decision support.

  7. STAGE 7

    System Integration

    Consolidation of the four individual research components into a synchronized pipeline.

  8. STAGE 8

    Decision Support

    Delivering actionable guidance for workforce readiness, risk mitigation, and hiring appraisal.

Methodology Flow Overview

Linear stage-gate execution ensuring data validity, rigorous empirical evaluation, and explainable decision support outputs.
